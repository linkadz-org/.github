## About Limzy

Running ads across multiple platforms usually means multiple logins, multiple dashboards, and a lot of guesswork.
**Limzy removes that complexity.** Enter your website URL, and our AI analyzes your business, recommends the right channels, creates on-brand creatives, and launches your campaigns everywhere — from one place.

Limzy is built for teams who want results from advertising without needing to become advertising experts.

## How it works

| Step | | What happens |
|:---:|---|---|
| **1** | 🔍 **URL AI Diagnosis** | AI analyzes your site to understand your brand, products, and advertising goals |
| **2** | 🎯 **Channel Recommendations** | Get the best-fit ad channels for your business, with the reasoning behind each pick |
| **3** | 🎨 **AI Studio** | Generate on-brand ad creatives automatically, using your logo and colors |
| **4** | 👥 **Audience Targeting** | Launch with AI-recommended audiences by demographics, industry, and interests |
| **5** | 🚀 **Multi-channel Launch** | Launch once — run everywhere |

## After launch, Limzy keeps working

- **⏱ Smart scheduling** — learns when your audience responds best and runs ads at the right times
- **💰 Automated budget optimization** — shifts spend toward high-performing channels and away from weak ones
- **📊 Unified dashboard** — see status and performance across every channel in one view, with AI recommendations on what to do next
